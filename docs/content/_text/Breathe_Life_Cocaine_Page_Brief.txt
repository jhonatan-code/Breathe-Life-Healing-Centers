UNDERSTANDING COCAINE ADDICTION
Cocaine addiction, sometimes called stimulant use disorder, often builds quietly around work, performance, and nights out before it becomes something harder to control. Once the body depends on it, stopping brings real withdrawal, not just a craving to push through.
Common signs of cocaine addiction
	•	Using more to chase the same high
	•	Withdrawal symptoms like fatigue, depression, and anxiety when not using
	•	Continuing to use despite wanting to stop
	•	Financial strain from sustaining the habit
	•	Risk-taking or paranoia while using
There's no FDA-approved medication built specifically for cocaine addiction. Some providers use disulfiram, more commonly known for alcohol treatment, off-label to help manage cravings and withdrawal. Our medical team can talk through what's appropriate for you.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Is cocaine withdrawal dangerous?
It's rarely life-threatening the way alcohol or opioid withdrawal can be, but symptoms like depression, fatigue, and intense cravings are real and hard to manage alone. Medical support makes a difference.
Is there medication to treat cocaine addiction?
No medication is FDA-approved specifically for cocaine addiction. Disulfiram is sometimes used off-label to help with cravings. Our medical team can discuss what's right for your situation.
I've tried to quit before and it didn't stick. Will this be different?
A past attempt that didn't hold isn't a sign you can't recover. It usually means the support around you wasn't matched to what you actually needed.
Will you address my anxiety or depression, not just the cocaine use?
Yes. Anxiety, depression, and trauma are treated as part of the same plan through our dual diagnosis approach, not as a separate concern.
Does insurance cover cocaine addiction treatment?
In most cases, yes. We're in-network with most major providers and verify your specific benefits before you commit to anything. We do not currently accept Medi-Cal or Medicare.
Is this treatment only for LGBTQ+ clients?
No. We're built for the LGBTQ+ community first, and that shapes how we treat everyone, but our doors are open to anyone whose values align with how we operate.
