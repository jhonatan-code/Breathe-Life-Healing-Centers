WHY FAMILY, ON OUR TERMS
Addiction doesn't happen in isolation, and neither does recovery. But “family” in treatment too often means a narrow, assumed shape — married parents, biological children, a household that looks a certain way.
That's not how it works here. Partners, chosen family, close friends, and community members are all valid participants in the family system. For a lot of the people we treat, biological family is absent, harmful, or estranged — often because of religious trauma, rejection, or a lack of affirmation. We don't treat that as a gap to work around. We treat it as the reality it is.
REAL EVIDENCE: our own call data includes a family member calling to arrange residential placement for her adult child's partner — not a spouse, not a blood relative. This principle isn't aspirational language; it's already how people are using this program.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Does “family” have to mean blood relatives?
No. Partners, chosen family, close friends, and community members are all welcome to participate — we don't require a biological or legal relationship.
What if my loved one's biological family isn't involved, or isn't safe to involve?
That's common, and it doesn't limit access to family programming. Many clients build their family system here around chosen family instead.
What's the difference between Family Class and the Family Workshop?
Family Class is focused, ongoing education and process work. The Family Workshop is a structured 3-day intensive for families ready for a deeper, immersive commitment. Many families do both at different points.
Can family participate remotely if they're not local to Los Angeles?
Reach out to our admissions team to talk through what's available — remote participation options vary by program component.
Is family participation required?
No, but it's strongly encouraged where it's safe and appropriate. Treatment moves forward either way.
Does insurance cover family programming?
In most cases family therapy sessions are covered as part of the overall treatment plan. Our team verifies your specific benefits before you commit to anything.
