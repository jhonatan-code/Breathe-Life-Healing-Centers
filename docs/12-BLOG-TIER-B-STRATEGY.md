# Tier B — content strategy for the 13 brand-strategy posts

_2026-09-11. Follow-up to `docs/11-REDIRECTS-GAPFILL.md`. These 13 URLs get almost no
current traffic (44 combined 6-month clicks, one post at 14 clicks, the rest single digits
or zero), but every one of them sits inside Breathe's own stated differentiators — the
chemsex track and the LGBTQ+-first audience (`docs/brand/Breathe_Life_Brand_Brief.docx`).
Redirecting them away on a traffic read alone would mean the rebuilt site covers Breathe's
actual specialty *less* than the old one does today, right as `/signature-services/` is
being built out. That's the wrong direction for a client whose entire positioning is built
on this exact audience._

## Why these are underperforming, not irrelevant

Every post in this tier ranks page 2–6 (position 18–63) for its target phrase. That's not
"nobody searches for this", it's "this content isn't winning the search it's aimed at."
Two likely causes, visible directly in the URLs:

1. **Thin, generic titles competing in a crowded field.** `/addiction-treatment-for-gay-men/`
   and `/gay-addiction-treatment-in-los-angeles/` are two different old posts fighting for
   the same intent — split authority instead of one strong page.
2. **No internal linking from a hub.** The old site has no single "LGBTQ+ addiction
   treatment" pillar page these posts point back to. The new site's
   `/signature-services/lgbtq/` and `/signature-services/chemsex-treatment/` can be that
   hub — which the old site never had.

**7 of 13 are functionally duplicates of each other** — the same "LGBTQ+ rehab in LA"
intent, six different titles, none of them winning. That's the real finding here, not "low
traffic."

## Proposed approach

1. **Consolidate the 7 "LGBTQ+ general" posts into content that feeds
   `/signature-services/lgbtq/` directly** — either as sections of that page, or as 2–3
   focused supporting pages it links to (e.g., one page per real distinguishing angle:
   general LGBTQ+ affirming care, and a separate transgender-specific page), rather than
   seven posts splitting the same search intent seven ways. This is a rewrite, not a
   lift-and-shift — the old copy is generic ("tailored support," "inclusive and
   understanding care") where the brand brief itself calls for specific, lived-experience
   language.
2. **Keep the 2 transgender-specific posts as their own angle**, but merged into one
   real page under `/signature-services/lgbtq/` rather than two thin ones — "transgender
   treatment for addiction" and "welcomes transgender people" are the same reader.
3. **Fold `/how-minority-stress-drives-substance-use-in-lgbtq-communities/` in as
   clinical framing/credibility content** on the same page — it's the one post here
   already using the right register (minority stress is real clinical language, matches
   "systemic barriers as clinical material" from the brand brief's six differentiators).
4. **Retire `/celebrating-national-lgbt-history-month/`** — a seasonal/calendar post with
   effectively zero traffic (1 impression) and no lasting value; redirect to
   `/signature-services/lgbtq/` rather than keep as a dated, one-off page.
5. **The 2 chemsex posts feed `/signature-services/chemsex-treatment/`** the same way —
   `/chemsex-addiction/` is thin and can fold in directly; `/grindr-hookup-culture-and-
   substance-use-what-queer-men-should-know/` is a genuinely distinct, specific angle
   (chemsex within hookup-app culture specifically) worth keeping as its own supporting
   piece once rewritten, since it's exactly the kind of concrete, community-specific
   content the brand brief asks for instead of generic framing.

## What this needs before it can move to build

- **Real copy**, written to the brand brief's voice rules (no "lifestyle," no conflating
  chemsex with sex addiction, centre LGBTQ+ without positioning Breathe as exclusive to
  it, no heteronormative assumptions) — none of the old posts' copy should be lifted
  verbatim the way the Tier A articles can be; they need an actual rewrite, not just
  cleanup.
- **A decision on URL structure**: do the consolidated pieces live as sections within
  `/signature-services/lgbtq/` and `/signature-services/chemsex-treatment/` themselves, or
  as separate supporting pages those pages link out to? Affects the redirect targets for
  all 13 old URLs either way.
- **Content briefs**, same as every other page on this site — this tier doesn't get an
  exception to the "no invented clinical/brand copy" rule just because the old posts
  exist. The old URLs are a signal of what to write about, not a source to copy from.

Not resolved by this document: only a recommendation for how to use the topical demand
this tier already has. The actual page structure and copy are a separate build task once
briefed.
