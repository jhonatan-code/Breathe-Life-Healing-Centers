The team at Breathe Life Healing Centers is always working to improve our facilities and standard of care, and our offices are one of the latest examples. Now located on Melrose, we're better equipped to serve clients well and to support the health and safety protocols that matter to their care.

The third floor was built almost entirely from scratch, and it's unrecognizable from what it was only months ago. An empty space is now open and bright, with a seamless flow from one room to the next. The move began at the end of August: admissions, HR, and marketing packed up first, followed shortly by the clinical team, who welcomed clients into the new community rooms.

The new location includes a much larger reception area for seamless check-ins, a client lounge with couches and space to relax, and a kitchen just down the hall with real room to move. Altogether, it's a space that's bigger, more accessible, and more accommodating for the people we serve.

## West Hollywood campus

Breathe's residential campus is a secluded retreat tucked into the heart of Laurel Canyon. The property spans several acres and includes modern, comfortable homes, expansive outdoor space with natural wildlife and views, a spacious community dining room, an amphitheater, a pool, and biodynamic gardens.
